CHAN'S PICKS Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

CHAN'S PICKS respects customer privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected in connection with our eBay store and internal order management operations.

1. Information We Process

We may process information provided through eBay, including order numbers, order dates, purchased products, product variations, quantities, buyer usernames, recipient names, shipping destinations, postal codes, and relevant transaction amounts.

Shipping addresses may also be accessed when necessary for fulfilling customer orders.

2. How We Use Information

Personal information is used for processing and fulfilling orders, coordinating shipments, providing customer support, maintaining accounting and sales records, resolving transaction disputes, and complying with applicable legal obligations.

Our internal order management integration does not collect customers' eBay passwords or payment card numbers.

3. Third-Party Services

CHAN'S PICKS uses third-party services for its business operations, including:

  • eBay - Marketplace transactions and order management.
  • Google Sheets - Internal sales and order records.
  • Render - Hosting our internal order management application.

These services may process information across different countries in accordance with their respective service arrangements.

We do not sell customer personal information.

4. Information Security

We aim to protect personal information through reasonable technical and organizational security measures.

Access to internal order records is intended to be limited to authorized personnel involved in our business operations.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for order administration, accounting, customer service, dispute resolution, and applicable legal requirements.

When personal information is no longer required, it will be deleted or de-identified, subject to applicable legal retention obligations.

6. Customer Privacy Rights

Customers may request information regarding the processing of their personal data and request correction or deletion where applicable.

Requests will be reviewed in accordance with applicable privacy laws and legitimate recordkeeping obligations.

7. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, personal information, or privacy-related requests, please contact CHAN'S PICKS through the eBay messaging system.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business operations, service providers, or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be made available on this page.
